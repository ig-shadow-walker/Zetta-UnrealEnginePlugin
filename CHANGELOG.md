# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first release (beta)

A prebuilt release for **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel) on Unreal Engine 5.8. A prebuilt Windows build is coming
soon; until then, Windows users can build it from source.

- Sign in with your browser; the session is kept in your system's key store.
- **3D Studio:** Generate 3D, Smart 3D, Segment, Retopology, Smart UV, Texture, Auto Rig (UE5 Mannequin skeleton
  first) and the Image Studio, with live prices and your plan's options.
- **My Generations:** your library, with import, place in the level, drag into the level, send to a tool, fork and
  pipeline runs as one tile.
- **3D Pipelines:** the node canvas, Describe it, runs on up to 20 seeds, and importing their results.
- **The Zetta Agent:** an Unreal-specific agent that works in your level, Blueprints, materials and C++, checks its
  work in the viewport, makes 3D models and pictures, and runs on your Zetta account or your own API key.
